# Stage 2 — Keyword research and search-intent map

**Research date:** 2026-11-11. Regulatory facts below are a snapshot of that
date and must be re-verified when content is written.

## 1. Method and its limits

- **What was done:** live search-result review for about 20 Day 1 CPT queries:
  who ranks, what angle each result takes, which questions recur.
- **What was not available:** search-volume and difficulty data. The Ahrefs and
  Similarweb connectors failed to connect in this session, so **no volumes are
  stated anywhere in this document** and priorities are qualitative.
- **Primary sources not opened directly:** `ice.gov` and
  `studyinthestates.dhs.gov` are blocked by this session's network policy.
  Statements attributed to them come from search summaries of those pages and
  from law-firm and university write-ups.
- **Validation still needed:** Search Console queries for `educonnectusa.com`
  (it already ranks for Day 1 CPT terms), plus a volume tool, before content is
  commissioned.

## 2. What the search results look like

| Who ranks | Examples | Angle |
|---|---|---|
| Enrollment consultancies and directories | day1cpt.org, day1cptuniversities.com, goelite.com, day1cptpros.com, cptuniversities.com | Deep directories: one page per university, per region, per cost tier, per on-site requirement, plus news |
| Fintech and career content sites | prodigyfinance.com, novacredit.com, zolve.com, interstride.com, f1jobs.io, trackmyopt.com | "Is it legal", risks, comparisons |
| Immigration law firms | visaserve.com, brownimmigrationlaw.com, rnlawgroup.com, natlawreview.com | Rule changes, RFEs, denials |
| Universities' international offices | umich.edu, washington.edu, stonybrook.edu, yale.edu, nyu.edu | Official policy updates |
| EduConnect USA | educonnectusa.com (`/dba/`, "Day 1 CPT Explained", "Best Day 1 CPT Programs for MBA and STEM Students", "Day 1 CPT H1B Guide", "Day 1 CPT in Doctoral Programs") | Program promotion and guides |

Three observations drive the page plan:

1. **Almost every ranking page is commercial.** Search engines' own summaries
   of these results repeatedly warn readers to verify with a DSO or attorney.
   Sourced, dated, plainly caveated pages are the gap.
2. **2026 rule changes have reshaped demand** (section 3). "Is it ending",
   "new rules" and "is it legal" are now core queries, and competitor
   university lists have started hedging ("research candidates, not confirmed").
3. **EduConnect already owns Day 1 CPT content on another domain.** If this is
   a separate site, the two will compete (section 6).

## 3. Regulatory context that changes the keyword set

| Date | Event | Status as understood on 2026-11-11 | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Standing rule | CPT must be "an integral part of an established curriculum". One academic year of enrollment is required first, **except** for graduate students whose programs require immediate participation. This exception is what "Day 1 CPT" refers to. | In force | 8 CFR 214.2(f)(10)(i); USCIS Policy Manual Vol. 2, Part F, Ch. 5 |
| Standing rule | One year or more of full-time CPT makes a student ineligible for OPT. Part-time CPT does not count toward that limit. | In force | 8 CFR 214.2(f)(10)(i) |
| Standing rule | CPT is authorized by the school's DSO in SEVIS and printed on the Form I-20. No USCIS application. | In force | Study in the States (via search summary) |
| 2026-07-17 | DHS final rule replacing duration of status with fixed admission periods (FR Doc. 2026-14439), effective 2026-09-15. Preamble states the rule does not prohibit or eliminate Day 1 CPT. Reported to restrict moving to a program at the same or lower level and to shorten the grace period from 60 to 30 days. | **Blocked.** Nationwide preliminary injunction reported 2026-09-14 (D. Mass.). Appeal status not verified. | Federal Register; National Law Review; university international offices |
| 2026-08-12 | SEVP Broadcast Message 2608-01: reminder of DSO liability for CPT authorization. | Guidance, in effect | ice.gov PDF (not opened); National Law Review |
| 2026-08-24 | SEVP Broadcast Message 2608-02: Q&A narrowing "integral". CPT tied to an optional elective reported as no longer qualifying. One source reports litigation over the messages. | Guidance, in effect; litigation unverified | University of Michigan, Stony Brook, University of Washington |

**Consequence for the site:** every page that states a rule needs a visible
"Status as of {date}" line, a named reviewer, and a source list. One living
rule-tracker URL should hold the current status so that individual articles do
not contradict each other. (The existing EduConnect blog already has that
problem, per its own site inventory.)

## 5. Decisions on the 13 initial page ideas

| Initial idea | Decision | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Day 1 CPT | **Merged into the home page** | Directory home pages rank for the head term; a second page would compete with it |
| What Is Day 1 CPT? | Own URL | Clear definitional intent |
| Day 1 CPT Universities | Own URL | Distinct "which schools" intent |
| Day 1 CPT Programs | **Home page targets this phrase**; `/programs/` targets degree-level choice instead | The site name is the keyword, so the home page will rank for it regardless |
| Day 1 CPT Requirements | Own URL, combined with Eligibility | Same question, same answer |
| Day 1 CPT Eligibility | **Merged into Requirements** | Two pages would cannibalize each other |
| Day 1 CPT Application Process | Own URL | Process intent is distinct from qualifying intent |
| Day 1 CPT vs. Traditional CPT | **Section inside "What is Day 1 CPT"** | Results answer this inside definition pages; no separate result set observed. Split out later if Search Console shows distinct queries |
| Day 1 CPT vs. OPT | Own URL | Dedicated comparison pages rank |
| Day 1 CPT FAQs | Own URL, as an index of short answers that link to the detailed pages | Avoids repeating the same Q&A on several URLs |
| Day 1 CPT Master's Programs | Own URL | Distinct; carries the second-master's question |
| Day 1 CPT DBA Programs | Own URL, covering DBA + doctorate + PhD | One doctoral-level intent |
| Day 1 CPT STEM Programs | Own URL | Distinct because of the STEM OPT question |

**Added from research (not in the initial list):** legality and risk, H-1B and
green-card impact, rule-change tracker, MBA, two situational pages (after a
layoff, after OPT), glossary. Legality/risk and rule changes are among the most
contested result sets and the clearest trust opportunity.

## 6. Overlap with educonnectusa.com

EduConnect's existing site already targets several of these clusters:
`/day-1-cpt-universities/`, `/dba/` (also `/day-1-cpt-dba/`), `/mba/`, and at
least eight 2026 blog posts on Day 1 CPT, the duration-of-status rule and
H-1B. If Day 1 CPT Programs is a separate domain:

- Copy must be written fresh. Reusing EduConnect text creates cross-domain
  duplicate content.
- The two sites need a division of labour, for example: the new site owns
  neutral education (what, rules, risks, comparisons); `educonnectusa.com` owns
  EduConnect's own program and partner pages; each links to the other.
- The site must disclose who operates it and that the operator has commercial
  relationships with universities. A consultancy-run site presented as a
  neutral directory is a trust and compliance risk.

This is an owner decision and is listed as open in the stage 3 document.

## 7. Rules that prevent cannibalization

1. One primary keyword per URL, recorded in the CMS. The Java app will reject
   duplicates of primary keyword, title tag, meta description and H1.
2. A page may answer a neighbouring question in one short section, then link to
   the page that owns it.
3. Blog posts target long-tail and news queries only. A post that would target
   a cluster in section 4 becomes an update to that cluster's page instead.
4. Each FAQ entry is displayed in full on exactly one URL.
5. Deferred clusters (19–21) get no placeholder pages.
