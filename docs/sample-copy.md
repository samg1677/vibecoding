## Guide page: What is Day 1 CPT?

**Title tag:** What Is Day 1 CPT? How It Works for F-1 Students

- Breadcrumb: Home / What is Day 1 CPT?
- Heading (H1): What is Day 1 CPT?
- Status line: Status as of 11 November 2026 · Reviewed by [Reviewer name,
  role] · 3 sources

### Short answer

Day 1 CPT is Curricular Practical Training that an F-1 student is authorized
to begin in the first term of a graduate program, instead of after one full
academic year. Federal regulations allow this only when the program requires
immediate practical training as an integral part of its curriculum. The
school's Designated School Official authorizes it, not USCIS.

### Key facts

| Fact | Value | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Legal basis | 8 CFR 214.2(f)(10)(i) | 1 |
| Who can qualify | Graduate students whose program requires immediate participation in practical training | 1 |
| Who authorizes it | The Designated School Official (DSO), in SEVIS. It is printed on the Form I-20 | 3 |
| USCIS application | None for CPT itself | 3 |
| Effect on OPT | One year or more of full-time CPT ends eligibility for OPT | 1 |

### On this page

1. How Day 1 CPT works
2. How it differs from regular CPT
3. What changed in 2026
4. What to do next

### 01 How Day 1 CPT works

Curricular Practical Training (CPT) is work, an internship or a practicum that
is an integral part of an established curriculum. Most F-1 students must be
enrolled full time for one academic year before they can use it.

The regulation makes one exception: graduate students whose programs require
practical training immediately. "Day 1 CPT" is the informal name for CPT
authorized under that exception. It is not a separate visa or a separate
status.

### 02 How it differs from regular CPT

The rules are the same. Only the start date differs, and only for programs
that meet the exception.

| | Regular CPT | Day 1 CPT |
|---|---|---|
| Earliest start | After one full academic year | First term of the program |
| Who it is for | Undergraduate and graduate students | Graduate students only |
| Curriculum test | Integral part of the curriculum | Integral, and required immediately |
| Authorized by | DSO | DSO |

Table caption: Source: 8 CFR 214.2(f)(10)(i)

### 03 What changed in 2026

**Caution box — Schools are applying a stricter test**

In August 2026 SEVP sent two broadcast messages to school officials about CPT
authorization. Ask the DSO at any school you are considering how its program
meets the "integral part of the curriculum" requirement. See rule changes.

### 04 What to do next

1. **Check the requirements.** See what the program and the student each have
   to meet. Link: Requirements and eligibility
2. **Weigh the trade-offs.** Understand the effect on OPT and later filings.
   Link: Is Day 1 CPT legal?
3. **Confirm with the school.** Only the DSO can say whether a specific
   program qualifies.

### Common questions

**Is Day 1 CPT a visa?**
No. It is a type of work authorization available to some students who already
hold F-1 status.

**Does USCIS approve Day 1 CPT?**
No. The school's DSO authorizes CPT in SEVIS. USCIS may review that history in
a later filing.
