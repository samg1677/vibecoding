Short answer

Day 1 CPT programs are graduate degrees whose curriculum requires practical training from the first term, so an F-1 student can be authorized to work in a related role without first completing one academic year. The school authorizes it, not USCIS, and it carries trade-offs for OPT and later immigration filings.
